WHAT YOU NEED

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 15 or newer, a Gmail
account, and an AI key from openrouter.ai. AXIOM shows you how to get
the key. You pay OpenRouter directly: $5 of credit usually covers a
few hundred applications. If you don't have Google Chrome, AXIOM
downloads its own copy the first time you open it (about 1.5 GB).


HOW TO OPEN AXIOM THE FIRST TIME
================================

1. Drag AXIOM onto Applications, the folder next to it in this window.
   Wait until the copy finishes, then eject this disk image.

2. Open Applications and double-click AXIOM.

3. The Mac says "Apple could not verify AXIOM is free of malware".
   That is expected: Apple only checks apps from developers who pay
   it, and AXIOM's hasn't yet. It doesn't mean Apple found a problem.
   Click Done. (Do not click Move to Trash.)

4. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security.

5. Scroll down to Security. Next to "AXIOM was blocked to protect
   your Mac", click Open Anyway. Type your Mac password, or use
   Touch ID.

6. Open AXIOM again. If it asks once more, click Open or Open Anyway.

You only do this once. If you cannot find Open Anyway, double-click
AXIOM again and look again straight away: it is offered for about
an hour after each try.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

- "Operation not permitted" while copying, or AXIOM says it was not
  copied completely: drag AXIOM to the Bin, then drag it from this
  window to Applications again. Use Finder, not Terminal.

- On macOS 14 or older: right-click AXIOM, choose Open, then Open.

- Stuck? In AXIOM, choose Help, then Copy Diagnostics, and paste
  the result into a message to whoever gave you AXIOM. It has no
  passwords, keys or mail in it.

TO REMOVE AXIOM

Quit it, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and follow Settings,
Your account, Delete everything first if you want your data gone too.
