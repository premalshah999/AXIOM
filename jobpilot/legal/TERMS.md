## What AXIOM does

AXIOM runs on your own Mac. Using your profile and resume, it finds job postings, fills in job applications, and can submit them for you. When you turn on automatic sending, it can do all of this without asking you first, including:

- creating accounts on employers' and hiring platforms' websites in your name;
- accepting those websites' terms and policies in your name;
- answering application questions from what you saved, and from text written by an AI model;
- reading your mailbox for verification codes, links and replies from employers.
